# Financial Services - Marketing Intelligence for Finance

Marketing and content strategies specifically designed for financial services — compliance-aware, trust-first, and conversion-optimized.

## When to Apply

Use when working on marketing for:
- Fintech startups (payments, lending, investing, crypto)
- Traditional banks and credit unions
- Insurance companies and brokers
- Wealth management and investment advisors
- Personal finance apps and tools
- Accounting and tax services

## The Financial Services Marketing Difference

Financial marketing has unique constraints vs. other industries:
1. **Regulatory compliance** — claims must be substantiated, disclaimers required
2. **Trust is the product** — people are trusting you with their money
3. **Education before conversion** — longer consideration cycles
4. **High stakes** — poor financial decisions have real consequences

## Core Frameworks

### The Trust Pyramid (Financial)
```
Level 1: Credibility — Who are you? Credentials, team, backing
Level 2: Transparency — How do you make money? What are the risks?
Level 3: Social Proof — Who else trusts you? Specific results
Level 4: Guarantee — What if something goes wrong? Protection
Level 5: Simplicity — Is this easy to understand and use?
```

### Compliance-Safe Copywriting Rules
- Never guarantee returns ("you WILL make money")
- Always include relevant disclaimers for investment content
- Use "may", "could", "historically" for performance claims
- Include risk disclosures near any return mentions
- "Past performance does not guarantee future results" on investment content
- Check country-specific regulations (SEC, FCA, ASIC, etc.)

### Financial Content Pillars
1. **Education** (50%) — How money works, explain concepts
2. **Tools** (20%) — Calculators, templates, assessments
3. **Social Proof** (20%) — Customer stories, results (with disclaimers)
4. **Product** (10%) — Direct product marketing

## Email Sequence: Financial Product
```
Email 1 (Day 0): Education — [Key problem] explained simply
Email 2 (Day 2): Social proof — "How [similar person] solved this"
Email 3 (Day 4): Tool — Free calculator / checklist / assessment
Email 4 (Day 7): Objection handler — "I know what you're thinking..."
Email 5 (Day 10): Case study — Specific results with numbers + disclaimer
Email 6 (Day 14): Soft offer — "When you're ready, here's how we help"
Email 7 (Day 21): Hard offer — Time-limited or bonus-added
```

## Landing Page Framework (Financial)
1. **Hero**: Problem statement + credibility markers (not returns)
2. **Trust signals**: Regulatory license, security certifications, press logos
3. **How it works**: Simple 3-step process
4. **Social proof**: Customer testimonials with real names and details
5. **Risk/transparency**: Honest about risks and limitations
6. **FAQ**: Address top objections and concerns
7. **CTA**: Low-commitment first step (free trial, free consultation)

## SEO for Financial Keywords
- Educational keywords have highest volume: "how to invest", "what is a Roth IRA"
- Comparison keywords: "best [product] for [situation]"
- Calculator keywords: "[calculation] calculator"
- YMYL (Your Money Your Life) content requires: author credentials, sources, last updated date, expert review disclaimer
- Expertise, Experience, Authoritativeness, Trust (EEAT) critical for Google ranking

## Compliance Checklist
- [ ] Investment disclaimers on all performance mentions
- [ ] "Results not typical" or "individual results vary" on testimonials
- [ ] Licensed advisor review for tax/investment advice content
- [ ] Regulatory body membership visible (SEC, FINRA, FCA)
- [ ] Privacy policy covers financial data collection
- [ ] Terms of service accessible
- [ ] Risk disclosure prominent on product pages
