# Superpowers - AI Productivity Intelligence

Frameworks and techniques for unlocking superhuman productivity with Claude and AI tools. Covers prompt mastery, workflow automation, research acceleration, and decision-making enhancement.

## When to Apply

Use when someone wants to:
- Get dramatically more done with AI assistance
- Build AI-powered workflows for repetitive tasks
- Accelerate research, writing, or coding
- Make better decisions faster
- Learn new topics at 10x speed
- Automate content creation workflows

## The 10 Core AI Superpowers

### 1. Prompt Chaining
Break complex tasks into sequential AI calls where each output feeds the next. Never try to do too much in one prompt.

### 2. Role Stacking
Define Claude's role, expertise level, and constraints at the start of every complex prompt. "You are a senior [X] with expertise in [Y]. Your constraints are [Z]."

### 3. Structured Output
Always request structured output (JSON, numbered lists, tables) for information you'll process or extract.

### 4. The Devil's Advocate
After getting an answer, always ask: "What are the strongest arguments against this?" and "What am I missing?"

### 5. Synthesis Engine
Dump multiple sources of information into Claude and ask for synthesis: "Given these [N] sources, synthesize the key insights, contradictions, and gaps."

### 6. Template Factory
Build reusable prompt templates for recurring tasks. Parameterize with [variables]. Store in a library.

### 7. The Research Loop
Ask → Get answer → "What should I have asked instead?" → Ask better question → Synthesize.

### 8. Decision Matrix
For any decision: "List the top criteria, weight them 1-10, score each option against each criterion, recommend the winner."

### 9. Persona Simulation
"Act as my [ideal customer / board member / skeptical investor / legal expert] and review this [proposal / plan / contract]."

### 10. Knowledge Compression
"Summarize this [document/topic] as if you had 5 minutes to brief an expert who will make a decision based on it."

## Prompt Engineering Mastery

### The PERFECT Prompt Formula
```
P - Persona: "You are a [role] with expertise in [domain]..."
E - End goal: "Your goal is to [specific output]..."
R - Rules: "Follow these constraints: [list]..."
F - Format: "Structure your response as: [format]..."
E - Examples: "Here's an example of what I want: [example]..."
C - Context: "Background information: [context]..."
T - Think: "Before answering, think step-by-step about..."
```

### Power Prompts Library

**Research Accelerator:**
"Research [topic]. Provide: (1) Executive summary (3 bullets), (2) 5 key insights with evidence, (3) Top 3 open questions, (4) What experts disagree about, (5) What I should read next."

**Document Analyzer:**
"Analyze this [document type]. Extract: key claims, supporting evidence, assumptions made, what's missing, potential risks, and recommended next steps."

**Content Multiplier:**
"Take this [input content]. Create: 1 LinkedIn post, 1 Twitter thread (7 tweets), 1 email newsletter section, 1 blog post outline, and 3 headline variations."

**Meeting Prep:**
"I have a meeting with [person/company] about [topic]. Prepare: (1) Top 5 questions to ask, (2) What they likely want from me, (3) Potential objections and my responses, (4) My desired outcome and BATNA."

**Decision Framework:**
"Help me decide [decision]. Criteria: [list]. Options: [list]. Provide a weighted scoring matrix, recommendation with reasoning, and what could make the wrong choice the right choice."

## AI Workflow Automation

### Daily Workflow Stack
1. **Morning brief**: "Summarize what I need to know today about [your domain]"
2. **Email triage**: Paste inbox → "Prioritize, draft responses to top 3"
3. **Content creation**: Research → Outline → Draft → Edit (4 separate Claude calls)
4. **EOD wrap**: "Given today's work [paste notes], what are tomorrow's 3 priorities?"

### Content Creation Pipeline
```
Step 1: Research → "What do experts say about [topic]? Give me 10 insights."
Step 2: Angle → "Given these insights, what's the most contrarian/interesting angle?"
Step 3: Outline → "Create a [format] outline for: [angle]"
Step 4: Draft → "Write the full [format] using this outline: [outline]"
Step 5: Edit → "Edit for: clarity, conciseness, stronger opening, better CTA"
Step 6: Multiply → "Repurpose for: email, LinkedIn, Twitter thread"
```
